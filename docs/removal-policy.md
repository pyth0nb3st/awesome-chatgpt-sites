# Correction and Removal Policy

Creators and rights holders may request correction or removal with the removal-request issue form. A public issue is not required when private handling is appropriate; contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile.

Maintainers will verify the requester's relationship to the site or content, preserve only the minimum information needed to process the request, and respond as promptly as practical. A removed site may be noted in `archive.md` without copying disputed content. Broken, private, deceptive, or unsafe sites may be removed without waiting for a creator request.
